Starter Concepts

  • Stocks: Shares of ownership in a company.
  • Risk: The chance your investment changes in value.
  • Diversification: Spreading investments to reduce risk.
  • Volatility: How much a stock’s price moves up or down.
  • Long‑Term Investing: Holding investments for years.

About Fund your Future

Fund your Future is a student‑run educational project created to help young learners explore investing concepts safely. Everything on this site is designed to teach financial literacy in a simple, engaging, and beginner‑friendly way.

Fund your Future is a student‑run educational project. This platform is for learning only — no real trading or financial advice.
